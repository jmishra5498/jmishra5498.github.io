Privacy Policy for IFCT NutriTrack

Last updated: September 2026

IFCT NutriTrack ("the App") is developed by Dr. Jitu Mishra. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Data Collection

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All food composition data and diet survey entries you create are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to any server.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn about AdMob's data practices and your choices at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Permissions

The App may request storage/media permission solely to export and share PDF diet-survey reports that you generate yourself.

Children's Privacy

The App is a medical/nutrition reference tool intended for general and professional use and is not directed at children.
